Dog Care Guide

Feed: high-quality dog food based on age, size, and activity. Split meals into 2 servings daily, and keep fresh water available.

Care: provide daily walks, regular grooming, vet visits, vaccination, and training. Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions, and cooked bones.

Cat Care Guide

Feed: balanced cat food rich in protein. Feed measured portions 2 to 3 times a day and always provide clean water.

Care: keep a clean litter box, brush regularly, offer scratching posts, and schedule routine vet checks. Avoid milk, onions, garlic, and bones.

Hamster Care Guide

Feed: hamster pellets, small amounts of vegetables, and occasional seeds. Provide fresh water daily.

Care: use a secure cage with bedding, a wheel, and hiding spots. Handle gently, keep the enclosure clean, and avoid sugary or salty human food.

Parrot Care Guide

Feed: pellets, fruits, vegetables, and a few seeds as treats. Avoid avocado, chocolate, alcohol, and caffeine.

Care: give daily interaction, toys, flight space, and mental stimulation. Clean the cage regularly and ensure vet checkups for feather and beak health.
